Hello, I am Barbara 🙋🏻‍♀️

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at Université Côte d’Azur, working with the Kairos team, within the pole COMRED, laboratory I3S and Inria at Sophia Antipolis, France. My research explores Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) for modeling and simulation of Cyber-physical systems (CPS). I’m particuarly interested in robotics, and I’m also curious about telecommunications engineering, machine learning, and cloud computing. Outside the work, I enjoy connecting with people, practicing sports, meditating, and learning new languages.
