Hello, I am Barbara 🙋🏻‍♀️

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science at Université Côte d’Azur – I3S – Inria in Nice, working with the <a href="https://team.inria.fr/kairos/">Kairos</a> team, within the pole COMRED.
